This contains the Tkinter code I made for my chatbot (2nd artefact chosen for this study)

This app is designed for educational and supportive purposes. If you are in a serious crisis, please use the Instant Support feature within the app to find professional help or contact your local emergency services immediately.

**Quackity: Your Mental Health Companion**

**Quackity** is a lightweight, mobile-inspired desktop application built with Python and Tkinter. It serves as a supportive chatbot designed to provide users with a safe space to vent, check their emotional "pulse," and discover mindful tools like breathing exercises, curated playlists, and self-help resources.

## ✨ Features

*   **Pulse Check:** A quick 1-10 self-assessment that provides tailored support based on your current mood.
*   **Vent Space:** A space to type reflect and pour your thoughts out through journaling.
*   **Mindful Tools:** Instant access to curated Lofi music, podcasts, YouTube resources, and self-help book recommendations.
*   **Breathing Exercises:** Staggered, guided breathing prompts to help stabilize and ground you during stressful moments.
*   **Instant Support:** A built-in directory of international mental health hotlines based on your location.
*   **Clean UI:** Customized with `customtkinter` for a modern look, featuring a scrollable chat interface and responsive buttons.
